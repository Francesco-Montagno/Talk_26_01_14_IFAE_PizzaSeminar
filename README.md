# Quark mixing from muon collider neutrinos - Pizza Seminar at IFAE Slides - 14/01/2026

These are the slides from the talk I gave at the Pizza Seminar at IFAE.  

They are based on our paper [arXiv:2511.23288](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23288), written by [David Marzocca](https://arxiv.org/search/hep-ph?searchtype=author&query=Marzocca,+D), [myself](https://arxiv.org/search/hep-ph?searchtype=author&query=Montagno,+F), [Manuel Morales-Alvarado](https://arxiv.org/search/hep-ph?searchtype=author&query=Morales-Alvarado,+M), and [Andrea Wulzer](https://arxiv.org/search/hep-ph?searchtype=author&query=Wulzer,+A).

All slides were generated using the **Manim** package.

If you couldn’t attend or want to know more, feel free to reach out to me by email [fmontagno@ifae.es](mailto:fmontagno@ifae.es)—I’m happy to answer questions!
